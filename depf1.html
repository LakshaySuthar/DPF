Is Your Vehicle Experiencing These Clogged DPF Symptoms?

Ignoring a blocked Diesel Particulate Filter can lead to catastrophic engine failure. Look out for these critical warning signs.

High Fuel Consumption & Loss of Power

Your engine is choking. A blocked exhaust forces the engine to work harder, burning more fuel while delivering less power.

Vehicle Forced into "Limp Mode"

The ECU restricts engine performance to prevent severe damage. You'll be unable to accelerate past a crawl.

DPF Warning Light Illuminated

The most obvious sign. If the dedicated DPF light or check engine light is on, immediate action is required.

Excess Smoke & Diesel Smell

Plumes of black or dark grey smoke from the exhaust, accompanied by a strong, unburnt diesel odor.

Frequent, Failed Automatic Regenerations

If your vehicle is constantly trying to run a regeneration cycle but failing, the ash and soot buildup is too dense for the vehicle to clear itself. It requires professional machine cleaning.
